About Platform & ML Methodology

Technical architecture, data science pipeline, and transparency reporting.

Problem Statement & Objective

Airline ticket pricing in India exhibits severe volatility governed by dynamic yield management algorithms. Passengers often struggle to assess whether a current fare represents good value or whether booking earlier or with an alternative carrier yields significant savings.

AeroMetrics solves this by providing a unified machine learning intelligence dashboard that computes estimated ticket valuations, maps out 50-day booking trends, and identifies cheaper flight permutations.

Machine Learning Pipeline Architecture

1

Data Preprocessing & Encoding

Categorical variables (airline, source_city, destination_city, departure_time, arrival_time, class) are transformed via Scikit-Learn OneHotEncoder. Numerical features (duration, days_left, stops) undergo StandardScaler scaling.

2

Model Architecture (Random Forest Regressor)

Ensemble of 100 decision trees constructed to capture non-linear airfare dynamics and multi-variable interactions (e.g. cabin class vs airline tier, duration vs stop penalty).

3

Serving via Asynchronous FastAPI

High-performance ASGI backend serving REST endpoints for single-point prediction, 50-interval booking curves, and multi-airline alternative optimization.

Transparency & Dataset Timeframe Disclaimer

This platform operates exclusively on historical flight booking data collected between February 11, 2022 and March 31, 2022 (50 days). Predictions represent algorithmic estimations based on historical pricing patterns and must not be construed as live booking quotes or active airline reservations.
